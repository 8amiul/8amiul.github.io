Such a Long Time

October 17, 2026

Tags: my-thoughts daily-things

Hello! As you can see, I am back again, writing on my site. Ah, I haven’t updated it and it has been idk maybe 6 months? I just didn’t feel like writing about the stuff I was doing. A lot… lot of things have happened. This few months were a something new which I’ve never done before. I got in to a university. Now I’ve become a first year electrical engineering student at AUST. I’ve got new friends, new people around me, new goals and finally a new way of living life

I always wanted to do something with computers. I had the chance to take it but then I came up with a new plan and chose this subject. I actually am doing well, at least for now. For the last few months I’ve involved myself in the most productive stuff that my passion follows. However, now it seems overwhelming. I don’t want to be jack of all trades and master of none. Isn’t it better to be jack of all trades and master of one? I think so. Let’s see where I head towards next.

By the way, I am writing thin on the ThinkPad that I bought. It is ThinkPad L14. I always wanted to buy a used ThinkPad because it is so freaking cool. No other laptops match the vibe of it. It feels nice to write stuff on it.

I actually don’t know what to right. I used to write stuff once now I just don’t Currently, I have a lot of topics to write about. But, where can I find the energy? Oh, yeah.. I am not the same guy anymore. I have changed myself a lot. I had to change a lot of things and had to accept it. I think it is for the best.

Let us see where life takes me to! I don’t know when I will write again. So if there is something anyone wants to know then, just email me or just find me on instagram.
